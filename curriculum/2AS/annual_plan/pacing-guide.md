# 2AS — Foreign Languages — Pacing Guide

> Week-by-week teaching schedule for the 32-week academic year.
> 5 hours / week × 27 effective teaching weeks (excluding holiday breaks
> and the diagnostic week) = **135 hours**, matching the official Ministry
> annual time allocation.

---

## How to use this guide

- **Calendar weeks (W1–W32)** are *teaching* weeks. Map them onto your
  school's calendar by entering the start-of-week date in the **Date**
  column. The Ministry circular published each August fixes the actual
  national break dates.
- **Holidays** are taken from the official annual distribution:
  - Autumn break (*عطلة الخريف*) — 1 week, mid-trimester 1
  - Winter break (*عطلة الشتاء*) — 2 weeks, around end of T1 / start of T2
  - Spring break (*عطلة الربيع*) — 2 weeks, around end of T2 / start of T3
- **Hour split** is the default 5-hour week. Schools using a 2 + 1 + 1 + 1
  split should treat the 2-hour block as the *Read & Consider* or *Write
  It Up* phase, where extended writing time is most useful.
- **Lessons** marked `L1`, `L2`, etc. are 1-hour slots referenced in the
  per-lesson plans (`../lesson_plans/`).

## Buffer & flexibility rules

1. **If a lesson over-runs**, the lost time is taken from the next *Practice*
   slot, not from the *Write It Up* / *Write It Out* slots — the project
   work is sacred (Six A's, Authenticity gate).
2. **If a lesson under-runs**, use the bonus time for the differentiation
   bank in `_shared/differentiation/` (a struggling-learner reinforcement
   or an advanced-learner stretch task).
3. **If a national / school holiday falls inside a unit**, do not skip the
   project milestone — push the summative test by one week and absorb the
   loss from the closest review block.
4. **If learners need more time on grammar**, prioritise modal verbs (U2),
   the passive (U3), and reported speech (U6) — these three are the
   high-leverage transfer items.
