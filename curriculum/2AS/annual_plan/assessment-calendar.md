## Assessment philosophy (alignment with the Six A's)

> **Source-fidelity notice (priority-4 content).**
> The official Annual Distribution prescribes only three written
> trimester exams (*اختبارات الثلاثي الأول / الثاني / الثالث*); the
> phase quizzes, exit tickets, project rubrics, weights (10/30/30/30),
> and the trimester written-exam blueprint in this calendar are
> **pedagogical inference** consistent with the Six A's "Assessment
> Practices" pillar. Treat these as `pending verification from official
> teaching map` for any official-record use; replace freely with your
> school's prescribed weights and instruments.

The Six A's of Project Design (from the official Teaching Map) demand that
assessment be:

> "Exemplars at the start; clear milestones at each phase; timely feedback;
> structured self-assessment using clear project criteria the learner has
> helped to set."

This calendar is built around those four moves:

1. **Exemplars first** — every project starts with a model artefact (in
   `../worksheets/`) so learners know the target.
2. **Milestones** — every unit has 1 summative test + 1 project artefact.
3. **Timely feedback** — every lesson ends with a 2-minute exit ticket;
   feedback is returned within 48 hours.
4. **Self-assessment** — every project rubric is shared *before* learners
   begin, and the closing 5 minutes of the project includes a
   self-assessment row.

## Assessment types & weights

| Type | When | Purpose | Weight in trimester grade |
|------|------|---------|----------------------------|
| **Diagnostic** | Week 1 | Establish baseline (no grade) | 0% — baseline only |
| **Formative — micro** (exit ticket, observation) | Every lesson | Adjust on the fly | 0% — feedback only |
| **Formative — meso** (phase quiz) | Mid-unit | Confirm phase mastery | 10% (continuous-assessment column) |
| **Summative — unit test** | End of every unit | Certify unit competency | 30% |
| **Project rubric** | End of every unit | Authenticity-grade transfer | 30% |
| **Trimester written exam** | End of T1, T2, T3 | Ministry-style written exam | 30% |

> The 10 / 30 / 30 / 30 split adds to 100% per trimester. Schools whose
> regulation prescribes a different split (e.g. 50% exam + 50%
> continuous) should adjust the column weights accordingly without
> changing the assessment instruments.

## Trimester written exam blueprint (Ministry-style)

The trimester written exam lasts **2 hours** and follows the standard
Algerian Baccalauréat-style format used in 2AS:

| Section | % | Format | Targets |
|---------|---|--------|---------|
| Reading comprehension | 30% | Text 200–250 words + 6–8 items (true/false, find synonyms, find antonyms, refer the pronouns) | C3 |
| Mastery of language | 30% | Grammar (rewrite sentences, fill in gaps, match), vocabulary (word families, synonym banks) | C2 |
| Written expression | 40% | Choice between 2 prompts (descriptive / argumentative / report) — 100–150 words | C2 |

A reusable blueprint and 3 sample exams (one per trimester) will be
authored in **Phase 6**.

## Trimester grade compilation (worked example)

A learner's **Trimester 1 grade** is composed as:

```
T1 grade = (formative-meso · 10%) + (project · 30%) + (unit-test · 30%) + (T1 exam · 30%)

Example for a learner who scored:
  - Phase quiz U1: 14 / 20      -> 7 / 10
  - Phase quiz U2: 16 / 20      -> 8 / 10
                                   averaged: 7.5 / 10  -> 7.5 % of 10 = 7.5

  - Project 1 (Life Style Profile): 16 / 20  -> 24 / 30
  - Unit 1 + Unit 2 tests average: 15 / 20    -> 22.5 / 30
  - T1 written exam: 13 / 20                  -> 19.5 / 30

  Total T1 grade = 7.5 + 24 + 22.5 + 19.5 = 73.5 / 100 (or 14.7 / 20)
```

The same structure applies to T2 and T3.
