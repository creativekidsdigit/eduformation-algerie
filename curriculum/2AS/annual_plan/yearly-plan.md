## The competency spine

Every lesson, activity and assessment in this year is tagged with one of the
three communicative competencies defined in the official Teaching Map (full
definitions in [`docs/01-pedagogical-framework.md`](../../../docs/01-pedagogical-framework.md)).

| Code | Name | What learners do |
|------|------|------------------|
| **C1** | Interaction | Negotiate meaning live (ask, answer, justify, agree, disagree, request, instruct) |
| **C2** | Production | Produce extended oral or written text for a purpose and an audience — every unit project sits here |
| **C3** | Interpretation | Extract, infer and interpret meaning from oral or written input |

## The six units at a glance

| # | Textbook unit | Annual-distribution theme | Project / portfolio | Weeks |
|---|---------------|---------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | *Signs of the Time* | Diversity / Lifestyle change | Life Style Profile (past · present · future) | T1, weeks 2–5 |
| 2 | *Make Peace!* | Peace and Conflict Resolution | Statement of achievements about Nobel Peace Prize winners + UNICEF speech | T1 wk 7 → T2 wk 13 |
| 3 | *Waste Not Want Not* | Poverty and World Resources | Conservation Plan (fact sheet · diagrams · town code · ideal-future map) | T2 weeks 16–17 |
| 4 | *Budding Scientist* | Technology and Innovation | Reports on scientific experiments + ABC of Dreams | T2 weeks 18–21 |
| 7 | *Science or Fiction* | Fiction or Reality? | Miscellanies (type-3 conditional statements · dialogue · biographical commentary) | T3 weeks 24–26 |
| 6 | *No Man Is an Island* | Disasters and Safety | Survey on disaster preparedness (questionnaire · interview · report with graph) | T3 weeks 29–31 |

Content weeks: **22** (U1=4, U2=6, U3=2, U4=4, U7=3, U6=3) × 5 h = **110 h.**
Diagnostic + 2 trimester-end reviews + 3 trimester exams: **5 weeks × 5 h = 25 h.**
**Year total: 27 teaching weeks × 5 h = 135 h.** ✔

## Teacher's mandate (non-negotiable, from the official Teaching Map)

1. **Project-based.** Every unit produces a project / portfolio artefact —
   activities are *judiciously selected so as to target the project* (source
   wording).
2. **Six A's of Project Design** — Authenticity · Academic Rigor · Applied
   Learning · Active Exploration · Adult Connections · Assessment Practices.
3. **Engage – Study – Activate** lesson cycle (default 60-min split:
   10 / 25 / 20 / 5 min closure).
4. **Six unit phases** — Listen & Consider · Read & Consider · Listening &
   Speaking · Reading & Writing · Write It Up · Write It Out.
5. **Integrated four skills** with explicit productive language listed for
   every task.
