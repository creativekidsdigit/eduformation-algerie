# 3AS — Third (Final) Year Secondary

**Textbook:** *New Prospects*
**Streams:** Letters / Philosophy (4 h/wk, 108 h, coef 3) · Foreign Languages (4 h/wk, 108 h, **coef 5**) · Sciences-Maths-TM (3 h/wk, 81 h, coef 2) · Mgmt & Economy (3 h/wk, 81 h, coef 2)

3AS is **the Baccalauréat year**: each stream studies 4 of the 6 textbook units.

| # | Unit | Theme | Project |
|---|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Exploring the Past | Ancient Civilisations | Expository essay on threats to civilisation |
| 2 | Ill Gotten Gains Never Prosper | Ethics in Business | Anti-corruption statement + opinion article + speech |
| 3 | Schools — Different and Alike | Education in the World | Wish poem + comparison letter + expository article on stress |
| 4 | Safety First | Advertising / Consumers / Safety | Advertisement + letter of complaint |
| 5 | It's a Giant Leap for Mankind | Astronomy & Solar System | Argumentative / expository newspaper article |
| 6 | We Are a Family | Feelings & Emotions | Letter of advice + film/book review |

## Stream-by-stream coverage

| Stream | Units covered (in order) |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Letters / Philosophy | 1 → 2 → 3 → 6 |
| Foreign Languages | 1 → 2 → 3 → 6 |
| Sciences / Maths / TM / Mgmt-Econ | 2 → 4 → 5 → 6 |
