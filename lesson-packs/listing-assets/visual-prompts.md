## 5. Branding consistency checklist *(apply to every visual you generate)*

- [ ] Uses the brand palette (deep teal · Algerian gold · warm ivory · charcoal · soft brick only)
- [ ] Uses Playfair Display (or Merriweather) for titles, Lato (or Open Sans) for body, Montserrat for badges
- [ ] No clip-art classroom imagery (red-pencils, smiley apples, comic-sans warnings)
- [ ] No dated stock photos (avoid 2010-era classroom photography)
- [ ] Algerian / Maghreb cultural cues subtle but present (haïk silhouettes, Arabic-calligraphy hints, Algerian flag pin in mockups) — never tokenistic
- [ ] All four product visuals (3 + bundle) feel like *one product line*, not four separate brands
