## 4. Visual Packaging Plan

### 4.1 Branding style guide

A single coherent look across all three covers + the bundle thumbnail. Use the same palette and fonts everywhere; only the cover illustration changes.

#### Colour palette (recommended primary)

| Role | Colour | Hex | When to use |
|------|--------|-----|-------------|
| Primary deep | Deep teal | `#2C5F5D` | Main title bar, brand bar |
| Primary warm | Algerian gold | `#D4A24C` | Accents, hooks, "INCLUDED" badge |
| Background | Warm ivory | `#F8F4ED` | Pages, panels |
| Body text | Charcoal | `#1F2933` | All paragraph copy |
| Highlight | Soft brick | `#B05544` | Sale tags only |

This palette evokes **Algerian heritage + classroom warmth + professional credibility** — three signals that convert well in the educator-resource niche. The teal anchors trust; the gold gives it lift.

#### Alternate palettes (if the primary feels too traditional)

- **Modern educator:** sage `#7A9B7A` + dusty rose `#D4A5A5` + ivory `#FAF8F3` + charcoal `#1F2933`
- **Bold edu-tech:** deep navy `#1E3A5F` + warm orange `#E07A3C` + cream `#F5F0E8` + charcoal

#### Fonts

| Role | Font (recommended) | Free alternative |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Cover title | **Playfair Display** (700) | Merriweather Bold |
| Sub-title / pull-quote | **Lato** (400 italic) | Open Sans Italic |
| Body | **Lato** (400) | Open Sans Regular |
| Tags / badges | **Montserrat** (700 ALL-CAPS) | Bebas Neue |

Playfair Display + Lato is a classic editorial pairing that signals "quality educational resource" rather than "clip-art classroom".

#### Tone of voice (across covers, descriptions, social posts)

- Warm but not gushing.
- Practical first, inspirational second.
- Address the teacher directly: *"you"*, *"your students"*, *"your Sunday evening"*.
- Concrete numbers (60 min, 4 lessons, 5 min prep) over adjectives.

| 1 (main) | Cover with bold title + one-line hook | The cover sells the click |
| 2 | Inside-spread mockup — lesson-plan page + worksheet page side by side | Proves printability |
| 3 | Single worksheet close-up (Worksheet 1 reading) | Proves quality |
| 4 | Rubric / answer-key close-up | Proves "ready to grade" |
| 5 | "What's Included" infographic (large bullets, branded layout) | Recap for skim-readers |
| 6 *(optional)* | A real-classroom photo or a testimonial card | Trust |

Use the same template across all three products so they stack well on the buyer's screen.
