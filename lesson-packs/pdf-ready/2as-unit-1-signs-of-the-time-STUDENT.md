# Vocabulary Reference (15 words)

> Print one copy per student. Keep this on hand throughout the unit.

| Word | Type | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| fashion | noun | popular style of clothes or hair |
| tradition | noun | a custom passed from one generation to the next |
| custom | noun | a usual way of doing something in a society |
| habit | noun | something a person does regularly |
| modern | adjective | of the present or recent times |
| ancient | adjective | very old; from a long time ago |
| lifestyle | noun | the way a person or group lives |
| entertainment | noun | activities people do for fun |
| ago | adverb | in the past (e.g. *ten years ago*) |
| nowadays | adverb | in the present time |
| invent | verb | to create something that did not exist before |
| discover | verb | to find or learn about something for the first time |
| predict | verb | to say what will happen in the future |
| progress | noun | forward movement; improvement |
| change | noun / verb | becoming different |

### Certainty Scale (read first)

```
100% sure   ----- will / are going to -----   I am sure ...
70% sure    -------------- probably -------   It's likely that ...
50% sure    ---- may / might / could -----   Maybe / Perhaps ...
```

### Task A — Place each prediction on the scale

For each sentence, choose the modal that fits your level of certainty (`will`, `are going to`, `may`, `might`):

1. People ___ travel to Mars one day.
2. Cars ___ fly in the sky.
3. Phones ___ be smaller and faster.
4. Schools ___ use paper books.
5. Robots ___ cook in our kitchens.
6. People ___ live to be 150 years old.
