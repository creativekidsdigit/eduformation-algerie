## Fidelity policy

1. **No invention.** Competencies, themes, units, projects, productive-
   language items, vocabulary glossaries, time allocations, coefficients,
   and stream divisions are extracted directly from the documents above.
2. **Pedagogical scaffolding is labelled.** Where the curriculum adds
   material the source documents do not specify (e.g. lesson-by-lesson
   timing, Bloom's-aligned objective wording, rubric descriptors, ADHD
   strategies), that material is marked as *pedagogical scaffolding* and is
   tied to an official competency.
3. **Gaps are surfaced, not filled silently.** When a source document is
   missing a piece (e.g. 2AS Unit 5 has no teaching-map page), this is
   flagged in `docs/04-quality-assurance.md` and any reconstructed material
   is clearly labelled *(reconstructed — please cross-check)*.
4. **Bilingual fidelity.** The annual-distribution PDF is bilingual. Where
   helpful, the Arabic source phrasing is reproduced (e.g. trimester names,
   stream names, holiday markers).

## What the .pps files contained

The three `.pps` slide decks proved to be image-based: the actual table
content is rasterised inside the slides. We extracted what little embedded
text exists and confirmed that the **two PDFs together cover all the
pedagogical content the slides summarise**. No information was lost.
